# CHEM 101 Topic 12: Introduction to Organic Chemistry — Problem Set

**Instructions:** Complete the following problems using the analysis method practiced in your guided assignment. For hydrocarbon problems, use the general formulas provided in the lecture. For biomolecules, identify the primary function of the molecule.

---

### Section 1: What Makes Chemistry "Organic"?

**Problem 1:** Determine if the following compounds are organic or inorganic: $\text{CH}_4$ (methane), $\text{CaCO}_3$ (calcium carbonate), $\text{C}_3\text{H}_7\text{OH}$ (propanol), and $\text{KI}$ (potassium iodide).

**Problem 2:** Why is carbon uniquely suited to be the "backbone" of organic molecules compared to other elements?

**Problem 3:** Compare the typical bonding type and state of matter for an organic molecule versus an inorganic salt.

---

### Section 2: Hydrocarbons

**Problem 4:** A hydrocarbon has the formula $\text{C}_4\text{H}_{10}$. Classify it as an alkane, alkene, or alkyne.

**Problem 5:** What is the name of a straight-chain alkane containing 3 carbon atoms?

**Problem 6:** Predict the molecular formula for an alkyne with 4 carbon atoms.

---

### Section 3: Functional Groups

**Problem 7:** Identify the functional group present in the following molecule: $\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CHO}$ (propanal).

**Problem 8:** Which functional group is characterized by the structure $-\text{COOH}$? Provide one example of a common substance that contains this group.

**Problem 9:** A drug molecule contains an $-\text{NH}_2$ group. What is the name of this functional group, and what is its general role in drug-receptor binding?

---

### Section 4: Isomers and Biomolecules

**Problem 10:** Two molecules share the formula $\text{C}_3\text{H}_6\text{O}$ but have different boiling points and react differently. What is the term for these molecules?

**Problem 11:** Match the following biomolecules to their primary function:
- Carbohydrates
- Lipids
- Proteins
- Nucleic Acids

(A) Genetic information storage
(B) Long-term energy storage and membranes
(C) Primary, readily available energy source
(D) Enzymes, structure, and transport

**Problem 12:** Explain the relationship between the sequence of amino acids in a protein and its final biological function.
