### Section 1: What Makes Chemistry "Organic"?

**Problem 1:**
- $\text{CH}_4$: Organic
- $\text{CaCO}_3$: Inorganic
- $\text{C}_3\text{H}_7\text{OH}$: Organic
- $\text{KI}$: Inorganic

**Problem 2:**
- **Answer:** Carbon has 4 valence electrons, allowing it to form 4 covalent bonds. It can also bond to other carbon atoms to form stable long chains, rings, and branches.

**Problem 3:**
- **Organic:** Typical bonding is covalent; often exist as liquids or gases (discrete molecules).
- **Inorganic Salt:** Typical bonding is ionic; usually hard, crystalline solids with high melting points.

---

### Section 2: Hydrocarbons

**Problem 4:**
- **Answer:** Alkane.
- **Reasoning:** It fits the $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+2}$ formula ($4 \times 2 + 2 = 10$).

**Problem 5:**
- **Answer:** Propane.
- **Reasoning:** Prefix "prop-" (3 carbons) + suffix "-ane" (single bonds).

**Problem 6:**
- **Answer:** $\text{C}_4\text{H}_6$.
- **Reasoning:** Alkynes follow $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n-2}$. For $n=4$, $2(4)-2 = 6$.

---

### Section 3: Functional Groups

**Problem 7:**
- **Answer:** Aldehyde.
- **Reasoning:** The $-\text{CHO}$ group is an aldehyde.

**Problem 8:**
- **Answer:** Carboxyl group. Example: Acetic acid (vinegar).

**Problem 9:**
- **Answer:** Amine.
- **Reasoning:** Amines allow the molecule to act as a base or form hydrogen bonds, enabling the drug to fit into a biological receptor (lock-and-key model).

---

### Section 4: Isomers and Biomolecules

**Problem 10:**
- **Answer:** Isomers.
- **Reasoning:** They share the same molecular formula but have different structures.

**Problem 11:**
- Carbohydrates $\rightarrow$ (C) Primary energy source
- Lipids $\rightarrow$ (B) Long-term energy storage and membranes
- Proteins $\rightarrow$ (D) Enzymes, structure, and transport
- Nucleic Acids $\rightarrow$ (A) Genetic information storage

**Problem 12:**
- **Answer:** The amino acid sequence determines how the protein folds into a specific 3D shape. Since function depends on shape (e.g., enzyme active sites), the sequence directly determines the protein's biological function.
