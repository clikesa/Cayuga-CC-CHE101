# CHEM 101 Topic 6: Names and Formulas — Answer Key

---

### Section 1: Naming Ionic Compounds (Fixed-Charge Metals)

**Problem 1:**
- **Answer:** Sodium fluoride.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Na}$ (Sodium) + $\text{F}$ (Fluorine $\rightarrow$ fluoride).

**Problem 2:**
- **Answer:** $\text{CaS}$.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\text{S}^{2-}$ balance in a 1:1 ratio.

**Problem 3:**
- **Answer:** $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Al}^{3+}$ and $\text{O}^{2-}$ balance $\rightarrow$ $2 \times (+3) = +6$; $3 \times (-2) = -6$.

---

### Section 2: Variable-Charge Metals & Polyatomic Ions

**Problem 4:**
- **Answer:** Sodium sulfate.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Na}$ (Sodium) + $\text{SO}_4^{2-}$ (Sulfate).

**Problem 5:**
- **Answer:** Iron(III) chloride.
- **Reasoning:** Three $\text{Cl}^-$ (-3 total) means the $\text{Fe}$ ion must be +3 $\rightarrow$ Iron(III).

**Problem 6:**
- **Answer:** $\text{Cu}(\text{NO}_3)_2$.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Cu}^{2+}$ and $\text{NO}_3^-$ (Nitrate) $\rightarrow$ need two nitrates to balance the +2 charge.

---

### Section 3: Binary Molecular Compounds (Nonmetals)

**Problem 7:**
- **Answer:** Sulfur hexafluoride.
- **Reasoning:** 1 Sulfur (no mono-); 6 Fluorines $\rightarrow$ hexafluoride.

**Problem 8:**
- **Answer:** $\text{CF}_4$.
- **Reasoning:** Carbon (1) + tetrafluoride (4 $\text{F}$).

**Problem 9:**
- **Answer:** Dichlorine heptoxide.
- **Reasoning:** 2 Chlorines $\rightarrow$ dichlorine; 7 Oxygens $\rightarrow$ heptoxide.

---

### Section 4: Acids, Salts, and Hydrates

**Problem 10:**
- **Answer:** Hydrobromic acid.
- **Reasoning:** Binary acid $\rightarrow$ "hydro-" + brom + "-ic acid".

**Problem 11:**
- **Answer:** Sulfurous acid.
- **Reasoning:** Based on sulfite ($\text{SO}_3^{2-}$), which ends in "-ite", so it becomes "-ous acid".

**Problem 12:**
- **Answer:** Barium chloride dihydrate.
- **Reasoning:** Ionic part: Barium chloride; Hydrate part: 2 $\rightarrow$ di + "hydrate".
