### Section 1: Naming Ionic Compounds (Fixed-Charge Metals)
*Reference: Slides 4-6*

**Problem 1: Entry Level (Binary Ionic)**
Name the compound $\text{LiBr}$.
- **Identify:** $\text{Li}$ is a Group 1 metal (+1); $\text{Br}$ is a nonmetal (Group 17).
- **Classify:** Fixed-charge ionic compound.
- **Translate:** Cation is lithium; Anion is bromine $\rightarrow$ bromide.
- **Result:** Lithium bromide.

**Problem 2: Medium Level (Writing Formulas)**
Write the chemical formula for Magnesium oxide.
- **Identify:** Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$) is Group 2 (+2); Oxide ($\text{O}$) is Group 6 (-2).
- **Classify:** Fixed-charge ionic compound.
- **Translate:** $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ and $\text{O}^{2-}$ balance in a 1:1 ratio.
- **Result:** $\text{MgO}$.

**Problem 3: Difficult Level (Aluminum Compounds)**
Write the formula for Aluminum sulfide.
- **Identify:** Aluminum ($\text{Al}$) is always +3; Sulfur ($\text{S}$) is Group 16 (-2).
- **Classify:** Fixed-charge ionic compound.
- **Translate:** To balance +3 and -2, the least common multiple is 6. We need two $\text{Al}^{3+}$ ions (+6 total) and three $\text{S}^{2-}$ ions (-6 total).
- **Result:** $\text{Al}_2\text{S}_3$.

### Section 2: Variable-Charge Metals & Polyatomic Ions
*Reference: Slides 7-9*

**Problem 4: Entry Level (Polyatomic Naming)**
Name the compound $\text{KNO}_3$.
- **Identify:** $\text{K}$ is potassium (+1); $\text{NO}_3$ is the nitrate polyatomic ion (-1).
- **Classify:** Ionic compound with a polyatomic ion.
- **Translate:** Potassium + nitrate. (Note: polyatomic names do not change to "-ide").
- **Result:** Potassium nitrate.

**Problem 5: Medium Level (Roman Numerals)**
Name the compound $\text{CuCl}_2$.
- **Identify:** $\text{Cu}$ is a transition metal (variable charge); $\text{Cl}$ is chloride (-1).
- **Classify:** Variable-charge ionic compound.
- **Translate:** There are two $\text{Cl}^-$ ions (-2 total). Therefore, the single $\text{Cu}$ ion must be +2. This is written as copper(II).
- **Result:** Copper(II) chloride.

**Problem 6: Difficult Level (Formula with Polyatomic)**
Write the formula for Iron(III) carbonate.
- **Identify:** Iron(III) is $\text{Fe}^{3+}$; Carbonate is $\text{CO}_3^{2-}$.
- **Classify:** Variable-charge ionic compound with a polyatomic ion.
- **Translate:** To balance +3 and -2, we need two $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ (+6) and three $\text{CO}_3^{2-}$ (-6).
- **Result:** $\text{Fe}_2(\text{CO}_3)_3$.

### Section 3: Binary Molecular Compounds (Nonmetals)
*Reference: Slides 10-12*

**Problem 7: Entry Level (Prefixes)**
Name the compound $\text{CO}_2$.
- **Identify:** $\text{C}$ and $\text{O}$ are both nonmetals.
- **Classify:** Binary molecular compound.
- **Translate:** 1 Carbon (skip "mono-"); 2 Oxygens $\rightarrow$ dioxide.
- **Result:** Carbon dioxide.

**Problem 8: Medium Level (Writing Formulas)**
Write the formula for Dinitrogen pentoxide.
- **Identify:** Di-nitrogen (2 $\text{N}$); Penta-oxide (5 $\text{O}$).
- **Classify:** Binary molecular compound.
- **Translate:** Combine the counts directly into subscripts.
- **Result:** $\text{N}_2\text{O}_5$.

**Problem 9: Difficult Level (Prefix Nuance)**
Name the compound $\text{P}_2\text{O}_3$.
- **Identify:** $\text{P}$ and $\text{O}$ are both nonmetals.
- **Classify:** Binary molecular compound.
- **Translate:** 2 Phosphorus $\rightarrow$ diphosphorus; 3 Oxygens $\rightarrow$ trioxide.
- **Result:** Diphosphorus trioxide.

### Section 4: Acids, Salts, and Hydrates
*Reference: Slides 13-16*

**Problem 10: Entry Level (Binary Acids)**
Name the acid $\text{HCl(aq)}$.
- **Identify:** Hydrogen + one nonmetal (Chlorine).
- **Classify:** Binary acid.
- **Translate:** "hydro-" + root (chlor) + "-ic acid".
- **Result:** Hydrochloric acid.

**Problem 11: Medium Level (Oxyacids)**
Name the acid $\text{HNO}_3$.
- **Identify:** Hydrogen + Nitrate ($\text{NO}_3^-$).
- **Classify:** Oxyacid (from an "-ate" ion).
- **Translate:** Nitrate ends in "-ate", so it becomes "-ic acid" $\rightarrow$ nitric acid.
- **Result:** Nitric acid.

**Problem 12: Difficult Level (Hydrates)**
Name the compound $\text{MgCl}_2 \cdot 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$.
- **Identify:** Ionic compound ($\text{MgCl}_2$) + 6 water molecules.
- **Classify:** Hydrate.
- **Translate:** Ionic part: magnesium chloride; Hydrate part: 6 $\rightarrow$ hexa + "hydrate".
- **Result:** Magnesium chloride hexahydrate.
