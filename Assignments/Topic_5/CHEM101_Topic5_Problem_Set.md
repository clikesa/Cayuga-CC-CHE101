# CHEM 101 Topic 5: Periodic Properties of Elements — Problem Set

**Instructions:** Complete the following problems using the analysis method practiced in your guided assignment. Show all your work, including your reasoning for trends and your electron dot diagrams.

---

### Section 1: Organizing the Periodic Table

**Problem 1:** Compare a period to a group. Which one contains elements with similar chemical properties? Explain why.

**Problem 2:** If an element is classified as a "metalloid," what does that tell you about its physical and chemical properties?

**Problem 3:** Why was Moseley's shift to atomic number more accurate than Mendeleev's use of atomic mass?

---

### Section 2: Periodic Trends

**Problem 4:** Between Sodium (Na) and Fluorine (F), which atom has a larger atomic radius? Justify your answer using periodic trends.

**Problem 5:** Between Lithium (Li) and Cesium (Cs), which element has a lower ionization energy? Explain why.

**Problem 6:** Compare the size of a neutral Magnesium (Mg) atom to its ion ($\text{Mg}^{2+}$). Which is smaller and why?

---

### Section 3: Ions and Charges

**Problem 7:** Predict the ionic charge for the following elements based on their group position:
a) Potassium (K)
b) Sulfur (S)
c) Barium (Ba)
d) Bromine (Br)

**Problem 8:** Why do the Noble Gases (Group 18) rarely form ions?

---

### Section 4: Electron Dot (Lewis) Notation

**Problem 9:** Draw the electron dot diagrams for the following elements:
a) Beryllium (Be)
b) Nitrogen (N)
c) Oxygen (O)

**Problem 10:** Phosphorus (P) is in Group 15. How many valence electrons does it have, and how many more does it need to complete its outer shell?
