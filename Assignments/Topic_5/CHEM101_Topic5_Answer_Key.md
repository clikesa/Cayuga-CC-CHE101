### Section 1: Organizing the Periodic Table

**Problem 1:**
- **Answer:** The Group.
- **Reasoning:** Elements in a group have the same number of valence electrons, which determines how they react chemically.

**Problem 2:**
- **Answer:** It has properties of both metals and nonmetals (e.g., may be a semiconductor).

**Problem 3:**
- **Answer:** Atomic number reflects the actual number of protons in the nucleus, which is the fundamental identity of the element, whereas mass can vary due to isotopes.

---

### Section 2: Periodic Trends

**Problem 4:**
- **Answer:** Sodium (Na).
- **Reasoning:** Atomic radius decreases from left to right across a period. Na is on the far left; F is on the far right.

**Problem 5:**
- **Answer:** Cesium (Cs).
- **Reasoning:** Ionization energy decreases down a group because the outer electrons are farther from the nucleus and more shielded.

**Problem 6:**
- **Answer:** $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ is smaller.
- **Reasoning:** Magnesium loses its valence electrons and an entire energy shell to become a cation.
