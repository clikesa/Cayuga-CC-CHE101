# CHEM 101 Topic 8: Chemical Quantities — Problem Set

**Instructions:** Complete the following problems using the analysis method practiced in your guided assignment. Show all your work, including conversion factors, molar mass calculations, and units at each step.

---

### Section 1: Avogadro's Number and the Mole

**Problem 1:** A sample of pure Silver (Ag) contains $3.011 \times 10^{23}$ atoms. How many moles of silver are present?

**Problem 2:** How many molecules are in $0.750 \text{ moles}$ of Ammonia ($\text{NH}_3$)?

**Problem 3:** How many **total atoms** are in $2.00 \text{ moles}$ of Sulfuric Acid ($\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$)?

---

### Section 2: Molar Mass and Mass-Mole Conversions

**Problem 4:** Calculate the molar mass of Potassium Nitrate ($\text{KNO}_3$).

**Problem 5:** A chemist weighs out $25.0 \text{ g}$ of $\text{KNO}_3$. How many moles is this?

**Problem 6:** How many grams of Magnesium Chloride ($\text{MgCl}_2$) are required to obtain $0.125 \text{ moles}$ of the compound?

---

### Section 3: Molarity

**Problem 7:** Calculate the molarity of a solution containing $3.00 \text{ moles}$ of solute dissolved in $1.50 \text{ L}$ of solution.

**Problem 8:** What is the molarity of a solution prepared by dissolving $11.69 \text{ g}$ of $\text{NaCl}$ (molar mass $58.44 \text{ g/mol}$) in enough water to make $250 \text{ mL}$ of solution?

**Problem 9:** An IV bag contains $2.00 \text{ L}$ of $0.050 \text{ M}$ Dextrose ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6$, molar mass $180.16 \text{ g/mol}$). How many grams of dextrose are in the bag?

---

### Section 4: Empirical and Molecular Formulas

**Problem 10:** A compound has an empirical formula of $\text{NO}$ and a molecular formula of $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4$. What is the multiplier?

**Problem 11:** A compound has an empirical formula of $\text{CH}_2\text{O}$ (molar mass $30.03 \text{ g/mol}$) and a measured molar mass of $90.09 \text{ g/mol}$. What is the molecular formula?

**Problem 12:** A compound has an empirical formula of $\text{C}_3\text{H}_5\text{O}$ (molar mass $57.07 \text{ g/mol}$) and a measured molar mass of $171.21 \text{ g/mol}$. Determine the molecular formula.
