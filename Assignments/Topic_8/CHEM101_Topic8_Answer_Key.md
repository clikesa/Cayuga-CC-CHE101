### Section 1: Avogadro's Number and the Mole

**Problem 1:**
- **Answer:** $0.500 \text{ mol}$.
- **Reasoning:** $3.011 \times 10^{23} \text{ atoms} \div 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ atoms/mol} = 0.500 \text{ mol}$.

**Problem 2:**
- **Answer:** $4.52 \times 10^{23} \text{ molecules}$.
- **Reasoning:** $0.750 \text{ mol} \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ molecules/mol} = 4.5165 \times 10^{23}$.

**Problem 3:**
- **Answer:** $1.45 \times 10^{25} \text{ atoms}$.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ has 7 atoms per molecule. $2.00 \text{ mol} \times (6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ molecules/mol}) \times 7 \text{ atoms/molecule} = 8.43 \times 10^{24}$... wait. 
- **Correction:** $2.00 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} \times 7 = 8.43 \times 10^{24} \text{ atoms}$.

### Section 2: Molar Mass and Mass-Mole Conversions

**Problem 4:**
- **Answer:** $101.10 \text{ g/mol}$.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{K}(39.10) + \text{N}(14.01) + 3 \times \text{O}(16.00) = 101.11 \text{ g/mol}$.

**Problem 5:**
- **Answer:** $0.247 \text{ mol}$.
- **Reasoning:** $25.0 \text{ g} \div 101.11 \text{ g/mol} \approx 0.247 \text{ mol}$.

**Problem 6:**
- **Answer:** $11.53 \text{ g}$.
- **Reasoning:** Molar mass $\text{MgCl}_2 = 24.31 + 2(35.45) = 95.21 \text{ g/mol}$. $0.125 \text{ mol} \times 95.21 \text{ g/mol} = 11.90 \text{ g}$.

### Section 3: Molarity

**Problem 7:**
- **Answer:** $2.00 \text{ M}$.
- **Reasoning:** $3.00 \text{ mol} \div 1.50 \text{ L} = 2.00 \text{ mol/L}$.

**Problem 8:**
- **Answer:** $0.800 \text{ M}$.
- **Reasoning:** Moles = $11.69 \text{ g} \div 58.44 \text{ g/mol} = 0.200 \text{ mol}$. Molarity = $0.200 \text{ mol} \div 0.250 \text{ L} = 0.800 \text{ M}$.

**Problem 9:**
- **Answer:** $18.02 \text{ g}$.
- **Reasoning:** Moles = $2.00 \text{ L} \times 0.050 \text{ mol/L} = 0.100 \text{ mol}$. Mass = $0.100 \text{ mol} \times 180.16 \text{ g/mol} = 18.02 \text{ g}$.
