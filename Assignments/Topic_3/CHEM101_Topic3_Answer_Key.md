# CHEM 101 Topic 3: Matter and Energy — Answer Key

---

### Section 1: States of Matter & Phase Changes

**Problem 1:**
- **Answer:** Gas.
- **Reasoning:** Only gases have no fixed volume and are highly compressible.

**Problem 2:**
- **Answer:** Sublimation; Energy is absorbed.
- **Reasoning:** Solid $\rightarrow$ Gas is sublimation, and moving to a higher energy state requires absorbing energy.

**Problem 3:**
- **Answer:** Dehydration reduces the amount of fluid available for sweat. Since the cooling effect depends on the evaporation of liquid water (which absorbs heat from the skin), less fluid means less evaporation and less cooling.

---

### Section 2: Classifying Matter

**Problem 4:**
- **Answer:** Compound.
- **Reasoning:** It consists of two different elements (H and O) chemically combined in a fixed ratio.

**Problem 5:**
- **Answer:** Heterogeneous mixture.
- **Reasoning:** The composition is non-uniform, as evidenced by the particles settling out.

**Problem 6:**
- **Answer:** Distillation.
- **Reasoning:** Distillation separates components based on boiling points; water vaporizes and is collected, leaving the salt behind.

---

### Section 3: Physical vs. Chemical Changes

**Problem 7:**
- a) Physical (dissolving)
- b) Chemical (digestion involves enzymes/acids breaking bonds)
- c) Physical (changing shape)
- d) Chemical (tarnish is a new substance/oxide)

**Problem 8:**
- **Answer:** Chemical change.
- **Reasoning:** A significant temperature change (release of heat) is a tell-tale sign that a chemical reaction has occurred.

---

### Section 4: Conservation of Mass and Energy

**Problem 9:**
- Calculation: $(12.0\text{ g} + 8.0\text{ g}) - 4.0\text{ g} = 16.0\text{ g}$.
- **Answer: $16.0\text{ g}$**

**Problem 10:**
- **Answer:** The total energy is conserved. The chemical potential energy stored in food is not destroyed, but is transformed into thermal energy (maintaining body temperature) and mechanical energy (muscle contraction/movement).
