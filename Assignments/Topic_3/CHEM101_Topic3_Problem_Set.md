# CHEM 101 Topic 3: Matter and Energy — Problem Set

**Instructions:** Complete the following problems using the analysis method practiced in your guided assignment. Show all your work and reasoning to receive full credit.

---

### Section 1: States of Matter & Phase Changes

**Problem 1:** A substance is highly compressible and fills the entire volume of any container it is placed in. Which state of matter is this?

**Problem 2:** Dry ice (solid $\text{CO}_2$) turns directly into a gas at room temperature. What is the name of this phase change, and is energy absorbed or released?

**Problem 3:** Explain why a person with severe dehydration may have a harder time cooling their body through sweating.

---

### Section 2: Classifying Matter

**Problem 4:** Is distilled water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$) an element, a compound, or a mixture? Explain your choice.

**Problem 5:** A suspension of an antibiotic medication must be shaken before use because the particles settle at the bottom. Is this a homogeneous or heterogeneous mixture?

**Problem 6:** Which separation technique would be most appropriate to remove salt from sea water to produce pure liquid water?

---

### Section 3: Physical vs. Chemical Changes

**Problem 7:** Identify the following as a physical or chemical change:
a) Dissolving sugar in hot tea.
b) Digesting a piece of bread.
c) Folding a piece of filter paper.
d) A silver coin tarnishing over time.

**Problem 8:** You mix two clear liquids in a test tube, and the mixture suddenly becomes hot to the touch. Is this a physical or chemical change? Justify your answer.

---

### Section 4: Conservation of Mass and Energy

**Problem 9:** In a chemical reaction, $12.0\text{ g}$ of substance X reacts with $8.0\text{ g}$ of substance Y. If $4.0\text{ g}$ of gas escapes from the open beaker, what is the mass of the remaining solid product?

**Problem 10:** Describe how the Law of Conservation of Energy applies to a patient's metabolism (the conversion of food into body heat and movement).
