### Section 1: Writing and Balancing Equations

**Problem 1:**
- **Reactants:** Methane ($\text{CH}_4$), Oxygen ($\text{O}_2$).
- **Products:** Carbon dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$), Water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$).

**Problem 2:**
- **Answer:** $2\text{Al} + 3\text{CuCl}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{AlCl}_3 + 3\text{Cu}$
- **Reasoning:** Balancing $\text{Cl}$ first (LCM of 2 and 3 is 6) requires 3 $\text{CuCl}_2$ and 2 $\text{AlCl}_3$. Then balance metals.

**Problem 3:**
- **Answer:** $2\text{C}_2\text{H}_6 + 7\text{O}_2 \rightarrow 4\text{CO}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$
- **Reasoning:** Balance $\text{C}$ (2 $\rightarrow$ 2), then $\text{H}$ (6 $\rightarrow$ 3 $\text{H}_2\text{O}$). Total $\text{O}$ on right is $4+3=7$. To balance $\text{O}_2$, we need 7 $\text{O}_2$ (14 atoms), so we double the $\text{C}$ and $\text{H}$ coefficients.

### Section 2: Types of Chemical Reactions

**Problem 4:**
- **Answer:** Combination (or Synthesis) reaction.
- **Reasoning:** Two simple elements combine to form one compound.

**Problem 5:**
- **Answer:** $\text{C}_3\text{H}_8 + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$
- **Reasoning:** Hydrocarbon + $\text{O}_2$ always yields $\text{CO}_2$ and $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ in complete combustion.

**Problem 6:**
- **(a):** Single Replacement. (Fe replaces Cu).
- **(b):** Double Replacement. (Ba and Na swap partners).

### Section 3: Solubility Rules & Precipitation

**Problem 7:**
- **Answer:** No precipitate.
- **Reasoning:** Potential products are $\text{NaCl}$ and $\text{KNO}_3$. Both are soluble (Group 1 metals and Nitrates are always soluble).

**Problem 8:**
- **Answer:** $\text{PbCl}_2(\text{s})$.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Pb}^{2+}$ is an exception to the chloride solubility rule; lead(II) chloride is insoluble.

**Problem 9:**
- **Answer:** $3\text{AgNO}_3(\text{aq}) + \text{K}_3\text{PO}_4(\text{aq}) \rightarrow \text{Ag}_3\text{PO}_4(\text{s}) + 3\text{KNO}_3(\text{aq})$
- **Reasoning:** Silver phosphate ($\text{Ag}_3\text{PO}_4$) is insoluble.

### Section 4: Clinical Applications & Synthesis

**Problem 10:**
- **Answer:** Double-replacement reaction.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Mg}(\text{OH})_2 + 2\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{MgCl}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$.

**Problem 11:**
- **Answer:** $\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2$.
- **Reasoning:** $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\text{PO}_4^{3-}$ require a 3:2 ratio to balance charges.

**Problem 12:**
- **Answer:** $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$
- **Reasoning:** Ethanol is the fuel; oxygen is the oxidizer; products are standard combustion products.
