### Section 1: Writing and Balancing Equations

**Problem 1:** Identify the reactants and products in the following equation:
$\text{CH}_4(\text{g}) + 2\text{O}_2(\text{g}) \rightarrow \text{CO}_2(\text{g}) + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}(\text{g})$

**Problem 2:** Balance the following equation: $\text{Al} + \text{CuCl}_2 \rightarrow \text{AlCl}_3 + \text{Cu}$

### Section 2: Types of Chemical Reactions

**Problem 4:** Classify the reaction: $2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{MgO}$

**Problem 5:** Predict the products for the combustion of propane ($\text{C}_3\text{H}_8$). Write the unbalanced equation.

**Problem 6:** Classify the following two reactions as either Single Replacement or Double Replacement:
(a) $\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4 \rightarrow \text{FeSO}_4 + \text{Cu}$
(b) $\text{BaCl}_2 + \text{Na}_2\text{SO}_4 \rightarrow \text{BaSO}_4 + 2\text{NaCl}$

### Section 3: Solubility Rules & Precipitation

**Problem 7:** Will a precipitate form if you mix aqueous $\text{NaNO}_3$ and aqueous $\text{KCl}$? Justify your answer using solubility rules.

**Problem 8:** Predict the precipitate that forms when aqueous lead(II) nitrate, $\text{Pb}(\text{NO}_3)_2$, is mixed with aqueous sodium chloride, $\text{NaCl}$.

**Problem 9:** Predict and balance the reaction between aqueous silver nitrate ($\text{AgNO}_3$) and aqueous potassium phosphate ($\text{K}_3\text{PO}_4$).

### Section 4: Clinical Applications & Synthesis

**Problem 10:** Antacids often use magnesium hydroxide, $\text{Mg}(\text{OH})_2$, to neutralize stomach acid ($\text{HCl}$). What type of reaction is this?

**Problem 11:** A common component of kidney stones is calcium phosphate. Write the chemical formula for calcium phosphate given $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and $\text{PO}_4^{3-}$.

**Problem 12:** Write the unbalanced equation for the combustion of ethanol ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$).
