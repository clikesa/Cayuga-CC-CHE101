### Section 1: Mole-Mole Calculations

**Problem 1:** Using the balanced equation $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{NH}_3$, how many moles of $\text{NH}_3$ are produced from $4.25 \text{ mol}$ of $\text{N}_2$?

**Problem 2:** Using the same equation ($\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{NH}_3$), how many moles of $\text{H}_2$ are required to produce $6.00 \text{ mol}$ of $\text{NH}_3$?

**Problem 3:** For the reaction $4\text{Al} + 3\text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$, how many moles of $\text{O}_2$ are needed to react completely with $2.50 \text{ mol}$ of $\text{Al}$?

### Section 2: Mole-Mass Calculations

**Problem 4:** Using $\text{N}_2 + 3\text{H}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{NH}_3$, how many grams of $\text{NH}_3$ are produced from $10.0 \text{ g}$ of $\text{N}_2$? (Molar masses: $\text{N}_2 = 28.02 \text{ g/mol}$, $\text{NH}_3 = 17.03 \text{ g/mol}$)

**Problem 5:** How many grams of $\text{Fe}$ are needed to produce $40.0 \text{ g}$ of $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$ in the reaction $4\text{Fe} + 3\text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$? (Molar masses: $\text{Fe} = 55.85 \text{ g/mol}$, $\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 = 159.69 \text{ g/mol}$)

**Problem 6:** In the reaction $\text{C}_3\text{H}_8 + 5\text{O}_2 \rightarrow 3\text{CO}_2 + 4\text{H}_2\text{O}$, if $150 \text{ g}$ of $\text{C}_3\text{H}_8$ is burned, how many grams of $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ are produced? (Molar masses: $\text{C}_3\text{H}_8 = 44.11 \text{ g/mol}$, $\text{H}_2\text{O} = 18.02 \text{ g/mol}$)

### Section 3: Molar Solutions

**Problem 7:** How many moles of $\text{HCl}$ are in $100 \text{ mL}$ of a $0.750 \text{ M}$ solution?

**Problem 8:** What mass of $\text{NaCl}$ is needed to prepare $250 \text{ mL}$ of a $0.300 \text{ M}$ solution? (Molar mass $\text{NaCl} = 58.44 \text{ g/mol}$)

**Problem 9:** $100 \text{ mL}$ of $0.200 \text{ M } \text{AgNO}_3$ reacts with $\text{NaCl}$. How many grams of $\text{AgCl}$ precipitate will form? ($\text{AgNO}_3 + \text{NaCl} \rightarrow \text{AgCl} + \text{NaNO}_3$; Molar mass $\text{AgCl} = 143.32 \text{ g/mol}$)

### Section 4: Percent Yield

**Problem 10:** If a reaction has a theoretical yield of $25.0 \text{ g}$ but only $21.2 \text{ g}$ is collected, what is the percent yield?

**Problem 11:** A reaction with a theoretical yield of $50.0 \text{ g}$ has a percent yield of $88\%$. What was the actual yield?

**Problem 12:** In the reaction $2\text{H}_2 + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$, $5.00 \text{ g}$ of $\text{H}_2$ is reacted. If $40.0 \text{ g}$ of $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ is actually produced, what is the percent yield? (Molar masses: $\text{H}_2 = 2.02 \text{ g/mol}$, $\text{H}_2\text{O} = 18.02 \text{ g/mol}$)
