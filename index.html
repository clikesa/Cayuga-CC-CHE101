Course Navigation Tips

  • Interactive Player: Use the "Repeat Slide" button if you need to hear a complex concept again.
  • Voiceover Experience: To make these presentations more interactive and engaging than simply reading slides, I've used an AI voice to model a supportive mentor. While we've tuned it for accuracy, you may notice it occasionally struggles with complex scientific terms or specific last names.
  • Guided Assignments: These are self-checking. Ensure you complete them before attempting the Problem Sets.
